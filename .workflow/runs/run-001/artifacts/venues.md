# Venue Research — 8th Birthday Party (run-001)

## Status
READY

## Purpose
Find and compare real, currently listed indoor venues in Greater London that can host ~20 guests
(mixed children + parents) on Saturday 14 November 2026, with parking or public-transport access,
within the overall £1,200 budget.

## Inputs used
- `artifacts/requirements.md` (location: Greater London, indoor, ~20 guests, near
  station/parking, fixed £1,200 all-inclusive budget, Saturday 14 Nov 2026 12:00–16:00)

## Content

### Selected: Magdalen Hall — Main Hall (Bermondsey, SE1)
- **Area:** Purbrook Street, Bermondsey, London SE1.
- **Capacity:** up to 120 (extendable to 150 with secondary hall) — comfortably covers 20 guests.
- **Indoor/outdoor:** Fully indoor community hall with a stage and kitchen (range cooker, fridge/
  freezer, microwave, kettle) — suitable for laying out the buffet.
- **Accessibility notes:** Not explicitly confirmed step-free; not a stated requirement for this
  event (see `requirements.md` — no accessibility need stated). Flagged as unconfirmed.
- **Parking/transport:** No on-site car park, but free on-street parking is available all day on
  weekends (the event is a Saturday), and the nearest station is London Bridge, a short walk/bus
  ride away — satisfies the "easy parking OR near public transport" requirement on both counts.
- **Estimated price:** Tagvenue lists the Main Hall from **£32–£35/hour** (two Tagvenue pages gave
  slightly different "from" figures); budgeted at **£35/hour, estimated**, for 6 hours (11:00–17:00,
  covering 1h setup + 4h event + 0.5h cleanup, rounded up) = **£210 estimated**. A refundable
  **£250 booking deposit** is also mentioned — this is a deposit, not a net cost, so it is
  **excluded** from the £1,200 running total but flagged for the family's cash-flow planning.
- **Constraints satisfied:** indoor ✅; guest count (20, well under 120 cap) ✅; parking-or-transport
  ✅ (free weekend street parking + London Bridge nearby); budget-friendly ✅.
- **Constraints not fully confirmed:** step-free accessibility (not required, but unconfirmed);
  exact hourly rate (source pages disagree slightly, treated as an estimate).
- **Source:** [Magdalen Hall Venue Hire — Tagvenue](https://www.tagvenue.com/venues/london/31300/magdalen-hall)
  and [Main Hall — Magdalen Hall — Tagvenue](https://www.tagvenue.com/rooms/london/62470/magdalen-hall/main-hall)
  — retrieved 2026-09-28. States location (near London Bridge), capacity, hire-from price, kitchen
  facilities, and weekend on-street parking.

### Alternative considered: Nastri Room, Firs Farm Community Hub (Winchmore Hill, N21)
- **Area:** Winchmore Hill, North London (N21).
- **Capacity:** up to 45.
- **Indoor/outdoor:** Indoor community hub room, used for children's play sessions and small
  parties.
- **Parking/transport:** No on-site parking, but free street parking on Firs Lane and side streets
  "throughout the day and night"; reachable via bus from Winchmore Hill Overground/Piccadilly Line
  (via Southgate) — satisfies the transport/parking requirement, but the tube/rail connection is
  less direct than Magdalen Hall's.
- **Estimated price:** £30/hour (Tagvenue listing) — cheaper than Magdalen Hall, but capacity (45)
  is a closer fit to the guest count and location is further from central London for most families.
- **Constraints satisfied:** indoor ✅; parking (free street parking, more generous hours) ✅;
  budget ✅ (cheapest option found).
- **Constraints not satisfied / weaker:** less direct public-transport link than Magdalen Hall
  (requires a bus connection rather than a short walk from a mainline/Underground station).
- **Source:** [Firs Farm Community Hub — Tagvenue](https://www.tagvenue.com/venues/london/39640/firs-farm-community-hub)
  and [Firs Farm Community Hub — official directions](https://hallbookingonline.com/firsfarm/documents/gHx2cD.pdf)
  — retrieved 2026-09-28.

### Alternative considered: "Play Base" all-inclusive kids' party package
- A 2-hour all-inclusive party package for up to 20 children, quoted at **£875** in aggregate
  research (venue + some activities bundled). This alone would consume roughly 73% of the total
  £1,200 budget for only 2 of the required 4 hours, leaving little room for catering, an extra
  activity, or contingency — **not selected** for that reason, but noted as a turnkey option if the
  family prefers to spend less on independent planning effort.
- **Source:** summarized from the aggregator page
  [Tagvenue — Kids Birthday Party Venues in London](https://www.tagvenue.com/hire/kids-birthday-party-venues/london)
  (retrieved 2026-09-28); the underlying "Play Base" listing was not independently opened, so this
  price is marked **unconfirmed/second-hand** and is not used in the budget.
