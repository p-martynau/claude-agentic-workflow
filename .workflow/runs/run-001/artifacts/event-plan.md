## Content

### 1. Event overview
An 8th birthday party for ~20 guests (mostly children aged 6–10, plus their parents), Saturday
14 November 2026, 12:00–16:00, Europe/London. Informal, fun, family-friendly atmosphere with no
specific theme. Fixed all-inclusive budget: £1,200 GBP.

### 2. Venue
**Magdalen Hall — Main Hall**, Purbrook Street, Bermondsey, London SE1. Indoor community hall with
a stage and kitchen, capacity up to 120 (comfortably covers 20 guests). Free on-street parking is
available all day on weekends, and the venue is a short walk/bus ride from London Bridge station —
satisfying the requirement for easy parking or nearby public transport. Estimated hire: £35/hour
for 6 hours (11:00–17:00) = £210 (estimated; a refundable £250 deposit is separate from this
running total). Step-free accessibility was not confirmed by the source and was not a stated
requirement for this event.

### 4. Activities
- **Children's entertainer show** (45–60 min): interactive magic/games/balloon-modelling show for
  ages 4–10, sourced from a live London entertainer-booking marketplace (from £100–£305 depending
  on act; £150 budgeted for a mid-range option). Not yet booked/confirmed.
- **Party games**: pass-the-parcel and musical statues, run by parents/host, no extra cost beyond
  small prizes.
- **Cake & candles moment**: the birthday centrepiece.
- **Free play / craft table**: supervised colouring/craft time for unstructured play.

### 5. Catering
Assumed ~14 children / ~6 adults (≈20 total).
- **Vegetarian option:** M&S Vegetarian Sandwich & Wrap Selection (20 pieces), £17.50 — explicitly
  vegetarian product line.
- **Nut-allergy-safe option:** Just Love Food Company "Happy Birthday Cake" ×2 (serves ~20 total),
  £18.00 — explicitly manufactured in a nut-free, peanut-free, milk-free factory.
- Additional buffet: M&S Kids Sandwich Platter ×2, £20.00; crisps/fruit/snack bowls, £30.00
  (estimated); juice/squash/tea/coffee, £25.00 (estimated). No alcohol, per the confirmed
  requirement.
- The standard M&S sandwich platters' own nut-allergen status was not individually confirmed;
  the nut-free guarantee for this plan rests specifically on the Just Love Food Company cake —
  double-check any other shop-bought item's allergen label before serving to a child with a nut
  allergy.

### 6. Services
- **Decoration:** number-8 balloon bouquet (£70 est.) + latex balloon bunch (£20 est.) = £90
  estimated, sourced from a London-wide balloon decorator; installed during the pre-event setup
  hour.
- **Party extras:** pass-the-parcel prizes and craft-table supplies, £20 (commodity items, no
  vendor needed).
- No photography, DJ, security, or transport service was requested or required for an event of this
  scale, so none is planned.

### 10. Open assumptions carried into this plan
- Guest split assumed as ~14 children / ~6 adults for quantity planning.
- Magdalen Hall's exact hourly rate (£32 vs £35 across two source pages) and exact bookable hours
  were not perfectly consistent across sources; the higher rate and a 6-hour block were used to
  avoid underestimating cost.
- The specific children's entertainer act has not been booked; a real, bookable marketplace option
  was identified and costed, pending the family's direct booking.
- Decoration and entertainer prices are "from" estimates; actual quotes may vary modestly, which
  the 10% contingency is intended to absorb.
