# Activities Plan — 8th Birthday Party (run-001)

## Status
READY

## Purpose
Propose kid-friendly activities and entertainment for ~20 guests (children aged 6–10 plus parents)
that fill the 12:00–16:00 programme, respecting the £1,200 overall budget.

## Inputs used
- `artifacts/requirements.md` (guest groups: children 6–10 + parents; must-have: kid-friendly
  activities; no theme/inclusions/exclusions specified beyond that; 4-hour window)

## Content

### 1. Children's entertainer show (sourced, external)
- **What:** A 45–60 minute interactive children's magic/entertainment show (magic tricks, balloon
  modelling, games) suitable for ages 4–10, run mid-party after arrivals.
- **Duration:** ~45–60 minutes.
- **Estimated cost:** £150 (mid-range option from the marketplace listing below; comparable
  named acts on the same page range from £100 to £305).
- **Suitability:** Designed for the 6–10 age range that makes up most of the guest list; also
  engaging for younger/older siblings who may attend.
- **Source:** [Poptop — Children's Magician listings, London](https://www.poptop.uk.com/london/suppliers/childrenentertainment/childrensmagician/)
  (retrieved 2026-09-28) — a live UK entertainer-booking marketplace listing named, bookable acts
  with starting prices (e.g., "Magic Dave's Magical Mayhem" from £150; "Ray's Children's Party
  Entertainment" from £100). The specific act has **not** been booked/confirmed as part of this
  plan — this is a sourced, budgetable option for the family to book directly.

### 2. Party games (generic program item, no external source needed)
- **What:** Traditional party games (pass-the-parcel, musical statues) run by parents/host,
  immediately after the entertainer's show.
- **Duration:** ~30 minutes.
- **Estimated cost:** No cost (uses items already needed for the party, e.g. a wrapped parcel with
  small prizes; a nominal ~£10 for wrapping/prizes is folded into the services "party extras" line
  in `services.md`, not double-counted here).
- **Suitability:** All children 6–10; simple enough for younger siblings too.

### 3. Birthday cake & candles moment (generic program item)
- **What:** Cutting the birthday cake, singing "Happy Birthday," candles — the must-have centrepiece
  moment of the party.
- **Duration:** ~15 minutes.
- **Estimated cost:** Covered under catering (`catering.md` — nut-free birthday cake line), not
  duplicated here.
- **Suitability:** All guests (children and parents).

### 4. Free play / craft table (generic program item, no external source needed)
- **What:** A simple supervised free-play and craft-table period (colouring sheets, stickers) to
  give children unstructured time after the structured entertainment, while parents socialise.
- **Duration:** ~45 minutes.
- **Estimated cost:** Nominal (~£10–£15 for craft/colouring supplies), folded into the services
  "party extras" line in `services.md`.
- **Suitability:** All children; low-supervision-intensity so parents can relax too.

### Venue/feature flags for schedule-builder and services-planner
- The entertainer show needs a clear floor area with the audience seated/gathered — Magdalen Hall's
  Main Hall (open floor + stage, see `venues.md`) supports this without extra AV equipment.
- No AV/sound system has been costed; the sourced entertainer listings are self-contained
  (bring their own kit), consistent with typical UK children's entertainer bookings.
