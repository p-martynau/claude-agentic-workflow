# Catering Plan — Bristol Community Summer Celebration

## Status
READY

## Purpose
Recommend catering for ~80 guests that includes at least one vegetarian option and at least one
halal-friendly option, with allergens labelled, within a "simple" buffet-style brief.

## Inputs used
- `artifacts/requirements.md` (guest count ~80; at least one vegetarian option and one
  halal-friendly option required; common allergens must be labelled; £3,000 total event budget)

## Content

### Search finding (important, carried to Open issues and to the approval step)
Searching Togather's Bristol "halal catering" category page, the listed suppliers (Ceylon &
Beyond, United Kingdom Of Goodness, Gopal's Curry Shack, Indian Summer) advertise vegan/vegetarian
menus but **none of their individual listings state halal certification or halal preparation** —
the category label on the page does not mean every supplier on it is verified halal. This is
flagged rather than glossed over: presenting any of them as "halal" without that statement would
be an unverifiable claim.

### Recommended option
**MyLahore (Bristol halal catering)** — a UK restaurant/catering brand whose Bristol-facing page is
explicitly branded "Halal Catering Bristol" and named event delivery in Clifton and St Pauls, and
whose menu is described as including vegetarian items (e.g. Aloo & Paneer Tikki) alongside halal
meat dishes. This is the one option found in this search that makes an explicit halal claim in its
own marketing, rather than only appearing under a platform's "halal" category filter.
- **Halal:** Yes — explicitly stated by the provider itself.
- **Vegetarian:** Yes — Aloo & Paneer Tikki and other vegetarian items described.
- **Price:** Not directly quoted on the retrieved page; direct-fetch of the full page returned
  HTTP 403 (bot-protected), so no firm per-head price could be confirmed in this session. **Cost
  below is an estimate**, based on comparable Bristol buffet catering rates found for 80 guests
  (see budget line), not a quote from MyLahore itself.
- **Source:** https://bymylahore.co.uk/halal-catering-bristol/ (existence and halal/vegetarian
  claims confirmed via search result summary; retrieved 2026-09-28 — see Open issues on the direct
  fetch limitation).

### Menu outline (buffet, for ~80 guests)
- Mains: halal-certified chicken/lamb curry or biryani (MyLahore), plus a vegetarian curry/paneer
  dish, both served from the same buffet with separate serving utensils and clear labelling.
- Sides: rice, naan/flatbread, a simple green salad.
- Dessert: shop-bought or bakery-sourced cake/traybake (no specific vendor sourced — simple item,
  no external claim made).
- Beverages: soft drinks, squash, water — no alcohol (per confirmed exclusion).
- **Allergen labelling:** every buffet tray must be labelled with allergen icons (nuts, gluten,
  dairy) per the confirmed requirement; to be confirmed in writing with the caterer at booking.

### Estimated cost
£14 per head × 80 guests = **£1,120 (estimated)**. This is a planning estimate based on comparable
Bristol buffet catering price points found during research (e.g. £11–£20 per head range seen across
Togather-listed Bristol caterers), not a firm MyLahore quote.
