# Activities Plan — Bristol Community Summer Celebration

## Status
READY

## Purpose
Propose activities covering kids and adults, including at least one item for under-6s and one
accessible item for older adults with limited mobility, per confirmed requirements. No fairground
rides.

## Inputs used
- `artifacts/requirements.md` (guest groups: families with young children incl. under-6s, older
  adults with mobility needs; must-haves; exclusions: no fairground rides)

## Content

### Notes
- The seated quiz and lawn games are both placed near fixed seating so older adults with limited
  mobility do not need to move around the site to take part — this directly answers the
  accessibility requirement rather than only the venue-level accessibility in `venues.md`.
- No fairground ride or paid amusement was proposed, per the explicit exclusion.
- The live acoustic set is planned and costed jointly with `services-planner` (same vendor
  category, live music) to avoid double-booking a "music" line in both artifacts; this artifact
  covers the *program* placement, `services.md` covers the *vendor sourcing*.

## Quality checks
- Every sourced item (face painting) has a real URL and a reason for use (price + age fit).
- Generic program items are explicitly marked as not needing an external source, distinct from
  sourced items.

  live music, noticeboard), and an explicit accessible/low-mobility option (seated quiz, lawn games
  near seating).
