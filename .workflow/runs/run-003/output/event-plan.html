Bristol Community Summer Celebration

Saturday 6 June 2027 · 13:00–18:00 · Trinity Centre, Bristol · Approved plan v2

Event Overview

An outdoor community summer celebration for around 80 local residents of all ages — families with young children and older adults included — at Trinity Centre (Garden + Graffiti Room), Trinity Road, Bristol BS2 0NW, on Saturday 6 June 2027, 13:00–18:00 (venue booked 11:00–19:00 for setup and cleanup). Casual, family-friendly community-fete style. No alcohol, no fairground rides.

Plan version 2 — Approved

Confirmed Requirements Summary

  • Guests: ~80 residents; families with young children (incl. under-6s) and older adults with mobility needs.
  • Budget: £3,000 GBP total, contingency included.
  • Must-haves: an activity for under-6s; an accessible, low-mobility-friendly activity.
  • Exclusions: no fairground rides, no alcohol sales.
  • Catering: at least one vegetarian and one halal-friendly option, common allergens labelled.
  • Accessibility: step-free access and space for pushchairs; limited on-street parking nearby.
  • Weather: a concrete rain contingency is required, since the event is outdoors.

Venue

Trinity Centre's outdoor Garden (25m × 20m) plus the adjoining indoor Graffiti Room (capacity 100 standing / 60 seated), booked together for £140 for the 8-hour day. An on-site 75-space pay-and-display car park resolves the limited on-street parking constraint.

Compared against Eastville Park (a Bristol City Council public park), which was not selected: events over 50 attendees need Council event licensing with a multi-month lead time, and no indoor wet-weather space is bundled with a park booking.

Weather & Contingency

6 June 2027 is too far ahead for a point forecast, so this plan uses a seasonal outlook built from real Open-Meteo historical data (six years, 2019–2024, 30 day-samples around 6 June, retrieved via the Open-Meteo MCP server): typically mild (mean 17°C day / 9°C night, range 13–22°C), with measurable rain on 18 of 30 sampled days (60%). This must be refreshed with an actual short-range forecast once the date is within about two weeks.

Contingency: the Graffiti Room is already booked as part of the venue, in the same building as the Garden. If a forecast checked 48 hours out shows ≥50% precipitation probability for 13:00–18:00, the event opens indoors from the start; otherwise it stays outdoors with the room held ready.
Decision-maker & guest communication: the residents' association's named on-the-day event lead makes the 11:00 go/no-go call and is the sole person authorised to call a mid-event move. If opening indoors, a notice goes up at the Garden entrance and on the community noticeboard channel by 12:30: "Due to the weather forecast, today's celebration is moving indoors to the Graffiti Room at Trinity Centre — same building, same start time, 13:00." If rain starts mid-event (sustained over 10 minutes, or heavy enough to affect the PA or buffet), the event lead makes one PA announcement and volunteers at the Garden's two access points help guests — including anyone with a pushchair or mobility needs — move indoors first.

Activities

  • Face painting (from £90) — children, including under-6s with a parent.
  • Parachute games & bubbles — children, no cost.
  • Lawn games (giant Connect 4, skittles, bean bag toss) — all ages, placed on flat ground near seating.
  • Seated garden quiz — accessible, low-mobility friendly, no cost.
  • Live acoustic music set — all ages, 15:00–17:00.
  • Community noticeboard / photo memory wall — self-serve, all ages.

No fairground ride or paid amusement was proposed, per the confirmed exclusion.

Catering

Buffet for ~80 guests with a halal-branded option (MyLahore) and a vegetarian option (paneer/vegetarian curry), served with separate utensils and allergen labels (nuts, gluten, dairy) on every tray. No alcohol.

Estimated at £14 per head (£1,120 total). No firm quote was obtained directly from MyLahore — their page could not be fetched in this research session — so this cost and the halal claim rest on the provider's own public marketing. The residents' association should confirm a written halal-certification statement and firm price before booking.
