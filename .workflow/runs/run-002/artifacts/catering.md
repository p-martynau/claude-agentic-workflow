### Recommended provider: Corporate Lunch (corporatelunch.co.uk)
Published list-price buffet tiers, confirmed to include vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and halal
options ("Everything is quality crafted in-house, with options available for vegan, vegetarian,
gluten free and halal sandwiches!").

- **Working lunch — Gold buffet tier, £20.95 per head (list price):** sandwich buffet with hot/cold
  items; provider confirms vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free variants available within this tier.
  40 x £20.95 = **£838.00** (list price, not yet a firm quote for this specific date/headcount).
- **AM tea/coffee + pastries, estimated £5.00 per head:** not a separately published line on the
  page fetched; estimated from the provider's individual-item price range (granola pots from
  £4.99) as a stand-in for a simple AM refreshment add-on. 40 x £5.00 = **£200.00 (estimated)**.
- **PM tea/coffee + biscuits, estimated £3.00 per head:** same basis, lighter afternoon
  refreshment. 40 x £3.00 = **£120.00 (estimated)**.
- **Delivery:** Free delivery on orders over £200 (this order qualifies) — confirmed on the
  provider's page.
- **Order lead time:** Orders before 2pm guarantee next-day delivery; for a date as far out as
  18 Nov 2026 this is not a constraint, but is noted for the internal team's ordering checklist.

**Dietary coverage:** vegetarian ✅ (stated), vegan ✅ (stated), gluten-free ✅ (stated). Northbridge
Analytics should still confirm allergen cross-contact handling directly with the provider once
final headcount and any additional allergies are known (per `requirements.md` open issue).
