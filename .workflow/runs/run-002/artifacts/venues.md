## Content

### Recommended: King's House Conference Centre — Sidney Street, Manchester M1 7HB
- **Capacity:** Online booking is listed for groups of 40 or fewer in its meeting-room
  configurations; the First Floor Hall separately lists capacity up to 300 for larger layouts, so a
  40-person seminar/meeting-room booking is well within range. *(Estimated fit — exact room
  assignment for 40 not itemised on the public page; confirm room name at booking.)*
- **Indoor/outdoor:** Fully indoor conference centre.
- **Accessibility:** Search-result summary of the venue's own accessibility page states step-free
  access to all main areas including meeting rooms, conference spaces, and communal facilities,
  with accessible toilets and a lift serving every floor — satisfies the stated step-free
  requirement.
- **Parking / transport:** Accessible on-street parking on Sidney Street plus local car parks a few
  minutes' walk away (satisfies "on-site or very close parking for a few drivers"); central
  Manchester location gives good bus/tram/rail links for the rest of the group (satisfies "good
  transit links"). *(Transit-link quality is a location inference from the M1 postcode, not a
  quoted claim from the source.)*
- **Wi-Fi / AV / flipcharts:** Listing states each room is equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, AV
  facilities, and comfortable seating — satisfies reliable Wi-Fi; a dedicated whiteboard/flipchart
  is not itemised on the public page and should be confirmed at booking (services.md separately
  plans flipcharts via the AV vendor as a fallback).
- **Price:** No firm day-rate for a 40-person room is published. Tagvenue lists a related room
  ("First Floor Hall") "from £48", which reads as an hourly or off-peak rate rather than a full-day
  40-person rate. **Estimated full-day (09:00–17:00, including AV vendor load-in/out access
  either side) hire cost used in this plan: £480, clearly marked as an estimate pending a direct
  quote** — based on the general Manchester conference-venue day-rate benchmark of ~£700/day for
  larger conference venues, scaled down for a mid-size seminar room at a lower-cost independent
  centre. Booking office: 0161 276 8194.
- **Constraints satisfied:** step-free access, parking for drivers, transit for others, indoor,
  guest count, Wi-Fi.
- **Constraints not confirmed:** firm day-rate; dedicated flipchart/whiteboard stock (mitigated via
  services.md).

### Alternative 1: Hyatt Regency Manchester — The Burgess Room
- **Capacity:** The Burgess Room accommodates 40 in cabaret style, 60 theatre style, or 50 seated
  dining — a strong capacity fit for 40 cabaret-style (better for group work than theatre rows).
- **Indoor/outdoor:** Fully indoor hotel conference space.
- **Accessibility / parking:** Hotel-standard accessibility expected (not itemised in the search
  snippet — would need direct confirmation); on-site hotel parking typically available at a hotel
  of this class, likely at a paid rate — not confirmed.
- **Price:** Not quoted in the source; hotel conference-space day rates in Manchester city centre
  are typically higher than an independent centre like King's House. **Not selected as primary**
  because King's House has a directly stated accessibility page match, and the Hyatt's
  accessibility/parking specifics are unconfirmed.
- **Constraints satisfied:** guest count/capacity, indoor.
- **Constraints not confirmed:** step-free access details, parking cost, price.

### Alternative 2: Manchester Marriott Hotel Piccadilly
- **Capacity/fit:** Hotel with 20 fully-equipped meeting rooms; a mid-size room should fit 40, but
  the search result does not itemise a specific room's capacity or accessibility/parking detail.
- **Not selected as primary** for the same reason as Alternative 1 — insufficient confirmed detail
  on accessibility and parking compared with King's House's directly stated step-free/parking page.
