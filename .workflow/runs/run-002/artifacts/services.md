### AV / equipment vendor: Event Production Group (EPG), Trafford Park, Manchester
Based at a Trafford Park warehouse, EPG provides AV hire and delivery throughout Manchester with
on-site project managers and technicians; equipment includes projectors (Sony/Panasonic,
5,000–20,000 lumen), projection screens, and PA systems/microphones "to suit any audience size."

- **Projector + screen hire:** estimated **£150.00** (no published price list; EPG quotes on
  request — this is a market-rate estimate for a single-room, one-day projector + screen package,
  pending a direct quote).
- **Wireless handheld/lapel microphone x2 + small PA system:** estimated **£220.00** (same basis —
  EPG's page confirms it offers this equipment class but does not publish a price).
- **Whiteboard/flipcharts:** provided by the venue where possible (see `venues.md` open issue); if
  King's House cannot confirm stock, EPG or a local flipchart-hire add-on would be needed as a
  fallback — not costed here pending venue confirmation.
- **Wi-Fi:** provided by the venue (King's House states each room has high-speed Wi-Fi) — no
  separate vendor cost.
- **On-site technician support:** included in the two estimates above per EPG's stated service
  model ("highly trained technicians" bundled with equipment hire).
- **Delivery, on-site setup, and collection fee:** estimated **£120.00 (estimated, pending EPG
  quote)**. *Added in this revision* — EPG's page states it provides "delivery throughout
  Manchester and surrounding areas" with "on-site project managers and technicians," which is a
  service distinct from equipment day-hire and is standard practice for Manchester AV vendors to
  charge as a separate line (covering the 08:00 load-in and 17:00 collection identified in
  `schedule.md`). The validator's `validation-report.md` v1 flagged that the original version of
  this artifact omitted this fee entirely; this revision adds it as its own costed line rather than
  folding it into the equipment estimates above, so it stays visible and auditable.

**AV/equipment subtotal: £490.00 (estimated, pending EPG quote).**
