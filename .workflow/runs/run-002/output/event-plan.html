Northbridge Analytics — Internal Strategy Workshop

Manchester · 18 November 2026 (±1 week if venue unavailable) · 09:00–17:00 · Approved

Overview

A one-day internal strategy workshop for Northbridge Analytics Ltd, held in Manchester for approximately 40 participants. The tone is business-casual and professional, with no theme. The full-day agenda is facilitated internally, supported by an accessible venue, a working lunch that meets all stated dietary needs, and a coordinated AV/equipment vendor so the internal team does not need to manage technical setup themselves.

This plan passed every applicable quality gate on its second validation pass. The first pass found that the AV vendor's delivery/setup/collection fee had not yet been priced into the plan; that gap was closed before this version was finalised. See "Services" and "Budget" below.

Confirmed requirements summary

  • Guests: ~40 participants, all adult employees, no children or VIP staging.
  • Schedule: Strict 09:00–17:00 window with a working lunch.
  • Accessibility: Step-free venue access required; on-site or very close parking for a few drivers; good transit links for the rest.
  • Dietary needs: Vegetarian, vegan, and at least one gluten-free option.
  • Technology: Projector/screen, microphone setup, reliable Wi-Fi, whiteboard or flipcharts.
  • Budget: £6,000.00 GBP total, with vendor-coordinated AV/equipment since the internal team does not want to self-manage it.

Venue

King's House Conference Centre, Sidney Street, Manchester M1 7HB.

  • Step-free access to all main areas, accessible toilets, lift serving every floor.
  • Accessible on-street parking on Sidney Street plus nearby car parks for drivers; central-Manchester transit links for everyone else.
  • High-speed Wi-Fi in every room; flipchart/whiteboard stock to be confirmed at booking, with the AV vendor available as a fallback.
  • Estimated full-day hire: £480.00 (estimate — no published day-rate for a 40-person room; confirm firm quote at 0161 276 8194).

Activities

Strategy sessions are facilitated internally by Northbridge Analytics at no external cost. A 15-minute mid-morning energiser is included at no cost using a self-facilitated icebreaker format. An optional paid alternative — a short conference-energiser session from Laughtercise — is available on request but is not included in the budget below pending a direct quote.

Catering

Provider: Corporate Lunch. Working lunch on the Gold buffet tier (£20.95/head, list price), with confirmed vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. AM tea/coffee + pastries and PM tea/coffee + biscuits are estimated add-ons. Free delivery applies (order exceeds £200). Allergen specifics beyond vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free are to be confirmed with the provider once final headcount is set.

Services

AV/equipment vendor: Event Production Group (Trafford Park, Manchester) — projector + screen, two wireless microphones + small PA, on-site technician support, and a delivery/setup/collection fee covering the 08:00 load-in and 17:00 collection. All AV lines are estimates pending EPG's firm quote. Wi-Fi is provided by the venue at no extra cost.
